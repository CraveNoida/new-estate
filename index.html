About the Founder

Trusted Real Estate Advisor

15+ Years of Real Estate Leadership

Bhaskar Rawat is a seasoned Real Estate Professional with over 15 years of experience across residential, commercial and investment real estate.

With extensive experience in the Gurugram and Delhi NCR markets, Bhaskar has also worked across Dubai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Pune, Chennai and Kochi, giving him a diverse understanding of real estate markets, evolving buyer preferences and investment opportunities.

His professional journey includes significant developer-side experience, along with expertise in property sales, investment advisory, residential and commercial real estate, and long-term client relationships.

“Real estate is not simply about buying or selling a property — it is about understanding a client's needs, evaluating the right opportunities and making informed decisions with clarity and awareness.”

A Mindful Approach to Real Estate

Beyond real estate, Bhaskar has been on a personal journey of mindfulness, meditation and self-awareness. His practice includes Vipassana meditation, along with regular meditation, pranayama and yoga. These experiences have shaped his perspective on life and strengthened his belief in being more aware, disciplined, balanced and conscious in the decisions he makes.

This philosophy also reflects in his approach to real estate.

The Conscious Realty Vision

Through Conscious Realty, Bhaskar's vision is to create a more thoughtful and transparent real estate advisory experience, built on trust, integrity, knowledge and long-term value.
